The problem

Traditional schools grade worksheets without showing what a young person can actually build.

Meanwhile, most digital credentials either lock learners into centralised platforms or expose minors on public blockchains.

What ARK Learning Lab does

One concrete piece of work, every session.

Learners aged 6–18 get a structured, project-based spine across three pathways: Core Academy, UnSchool and LIBERVERSE Learning Lab. Ten Practical Independence Skills run alongside, including repair, food growing, water sense, energy, navigation, first aid and trade. Every session ends with one piece of work filed in a private portfolio saved on the learner’s own device.

Blockchain V2

Designed with Liberland Blockchain V2 in mind

The ARK Proof Kit prototype fingerprints portfolio files locally in the browser (SHA-256). Its optional demo anchors an anonymous, parent-consented batch fingerprint on Ethereum Sepolia, a test network, using sample work. Blockchain V2 is planned to be Ethereum-based, so we want V2 builders to test whether it could take the same approach.

Constitutional Convention

Constitutional and civic literacy

Learners can study Liberland as an optional, critical-thinking civics case study. They compare Liberland’s constitutional drafts with Croatian and EU positions, and test majority, ranked-choice and quadratic voting in practice games.

Liberland Merit

A vetted mentor ecosystem

Learners earn printable certificates. They receive no crypto wallets, tokens or Liberland Merits. ARK is building a vetted roster of engineers, architects and policy practitioners to review capstone drafts. About mentors

Clear safeguarding and governance boundaries

What ARK is, and what it is not.

Independent and not accredited

ARK Learning Lab is not an official institution of the Liberland government and is not a state school. Families in Czechia, Slovakia and the EU can pair ARK enrichment with accredited online schooling, such as Hill Castle International School, which is separate from ARK.

Zero learner data on-chain

No student names, files, scores or locations are ever written to a blockchain. Parental consent is opt-in and can be withdrawn. Read the safeguarding plan

Honest readiness status

Enrolment and payments are not open yet; website pricing is draft only. Nothing runs at ARK Village in Apatin, Serbia yet. Cohorts would launch online and in local family pods first.
