In plain words

What LIBERVERSE is, and what it is not.

What is it?

A set of online lessons about the digital world: keeping information private, reading data, writing code, using AI carefully, designing a city and making fair rules. It is one of three ARK Learning Lab pathways.

Who is it for?

Learners aged 6 to 18, with a parent, guardian or pod facilitator. The learner picks one age group and works through its six modules, then a four-week capstone project.

Where does it happen?

Online, on a computer or tablet, with a print version of every module for screen-free days. Pods can meet at their own venue. Mentor meetings are online. Nothing happens at ARK Village in Serbia yet.

What does the learner end up with?

A private portfolio: one piece of work from each module, each designed to feed into a final capstone project shown to a mentor. The Proof Kit prototype shows how that work could later be checked without sharing the learner’s name.

Is it a school?

No. It is a draft enrichment programme, not an accredited school or qualification. Families stay responsible for local education rules.

Can we join today?

Not yet. Enrolment and payments are not open. You can read every module, inspect the 6-lesson rhythm, use the materials library and try the free tools now.

How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

  1. Pick the learner's age group.Each of the four age groups has its own six modules and tailored capstone project.
  2. Follow the age-fit sequence.Each module is 6 lessons (about one lesson a week; nothing is timed). Ages 6–11 can take modules in any order. Ages 12–18 follow a Recommended Sequence: complete Module 1 (Privacy) → Module 3 (Code) → Module 2 (Data) first so coding tools are ready before tackling Modules 4–6 in any order.
  3. Build Capstone Bridges.In Lesson 6 of every module, the learner files one completed artifact in their private portfolio as a direct building block for the final project, paired with their mentor's revision note.
  4. Finish with a 4-week capstone.After all six modules: a 4-week capstone project that pulls all six portfolio pieces together into one coherent build, shown to an online mentor.
Where LIBERVERSE happens, and print-first technical parity

Mostly online at home, on a computer or tablet. Every module also has a print version for screen-free days: print packs include unplugged algorithm puzzles and paper state machines for ages 6–11, and downloadable single-file starter kits (Python/HTML) plus paper code-design sheets for ages 12–18. Pods can run it at their own venue. Mentor meetings are online. ARK Village in Apatin, Serbia, is not open yet: no lessons or visit days run there now.

One year is about 40 weeks.

6 modules × 6 lessons = 36 lessons, plus a 4-week capstone. Go slower if you need to. Nothing is timed.

Pacing & Proof Kit alignment

90 minutes, adapted by age, plus a 6-lesson rhythm.

Every lesson follows the same four-part sequence, while pacing and output formats adapt to the learner's stage.

15 min · Learn

See the core idea

A short video, reading or audio explains one clear concept without jargon.

30 min · Try

Guided worked example

The learner copies or traces a worked example step by step.

30 min · Build

Learner-led creation

They work on their own project choice using digital or print tools.

15 min · Show

File & reflect

They file the work in their portfolio and record a short reflection.

The parent/guardian's job: set up the time and space, check the work is filed, and ask “what did you make today?” You don't need to know how to code. The mentor reviews the work online.

The 6-lesson module rhythm (Proof Kit ready)

Every module follows the same six-lesson arc so mentor feedback happens before final filing, in line with the ARK Proof Kit anti-rubber-stamp review rule.

Lessons 1–2

Core Mechanics

Short guided builds introducing the theme's core concept, vocabulary, and local/print starter tools.

Lessons 3–4

Applied Challenge

Learners apply the concept to a civic scenario or real river dataset (the Danube is one of five test rivers).

Lesson 5

Draft & Mentor Checkpoint

Learner submits a working draft; the online mentor logs one concrete revision suggestion (no pass/fail grades).

Lesson 6

Revise, Bridge & File

Learner implements the revision, completes the Show reflection, and archives both artifact and revision note for optional parent-consented Proof Kit signing.

Curriculum guardrails

How AI and governance progress across ages.

AI spine: verification over generation

Learners never treat AI output as an unverified answer. Starting at age 12 (Module 4), every project that uses an AI assistant must include a 3-line AI Audit Box in the portfolio file:

1. Prompt used: [exact question or instruction given]
2. Error or gap caught: [what the AI missed, invented, or oversimplified]
3. External check: [independent source or test used to verify]

Governance spine: mechanism comparison

Across The Class Market (9–11), Ledgers, Votes and Trust (12–14) and Constitutional Design Studio (15–18), learners test the same resource-allocation scenario under three rule sets (simple majority, ranked choice and quadratic or token-budget voting, on paper or in Python) to compare trade-offs and dispute resolution. All simulations are strictly educational practice games.

Child safeguarding

What we never do.

  • No crypto wallets, tokens or payments for learners. Ever.
  • No learner records on any blockchain. The Proof Kit prototype can add only an anonymous fingerprint to a test network, and only if a parent opts in: never a name, file, score or location.
  • Cameras are optional. Paper and audio work count.
  • All votes and ledgers are practice games, not official Liberland processes.
  • Learners under 13 use accounts set up and managed by a parent/guardian.
  • Every module has a print-friendly, low-internet version.

Every seat lets one learner choose LIBERVERSE Learning Lab, Core Academy or UnSchool. Prices are the same whichever pathway the learner picks: One Learner $490, mini Pod (up to 4) $890, full Pod (up to 10) $1,490, extra seats $200. Draft prices, not yet for sale.
