LIBERVERSE Learning Lab · full curriculum

Digital skills for real life, one module at a time.

Your child learns privacy, data, coding, AI, city design and how communities make rules. Ages 6 to 18. Online first, with print and ARK options.

Draft curriculum · enrichment, not accreditation

How it works

Four steps, start to finish.

  1. 1. Pick your child's ageThere are four age groups. Each has its own six modules.
  2. 2. Do one module at a timeEach module is 6 lessons, one a week, in any order you like.
  3. 3. Keep the workAfter each module your child keeps one thing they made in a private folder.
  4. 4. Finish with a capstoneAfter all six modules: a 4-week project that pulls everything together.
One year = 40 weeks.

6 modules × 6 weeks = 36 weeks, plus a 4-week capstone. Go slower if you need to. Nothing is timed.

What one lesson looks like

90 minutes, always in the same order.

  1. 15 min · LearnA short video, reading or audio explains the idea.
  2. 30 min · TryYour child copies a worked example, step by step.
  3. 30 min · BuildThey work on their own project choice.
  4. 15 min · ShowThey upload or file their work and write one sentence about it.

Your job as a parent: set up the time and space, check the work is filed, and ask "what did you make today?" You don't need to know how to code.

Safety rules

What we never do.

  • No crypto wallets, tokens or payments for children. Ever.
  • No child's records on any blockchain.
  • Cameras are optional. Paper and audio work count.
  • All votes and ledgers are practice games, not official Liberland processes.
  • Children under 13 use accounts set up and managed by a parent.
  • Every module has a print-friendly, low-internet version.
