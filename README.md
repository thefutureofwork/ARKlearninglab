# Liberland Academy

Public website for **Liberland Academy**, with **UnSchool** and **LIBERVERSE Learning Lab** pathways, the **ARK Learning Lab** six-week pilot, practical skills, and the **Liberland Science & Civics Pack**.

## Public pages

- [Home](https://thefutureofwork.github.io/liberlandacademy/): pathways, six-week pilot, practical skills, Science & Civics Pack, packages and plan builder.
- [Curriculum framework](https://thefutureofwork.github.io/liberlandacademy/curriculum.html): shared learning spine, stewardship, age-band progression, eight Science & Civics units, pathway and assessment model.
- [Safeguarding framework](https://thefutureofwork.github.io/liberlandacademy/safeguarding.html): proposed safety commitments and the pre-launch gates for any programme involving minors.

## Important status

The curriculum and safeguarding pages are working drafts. The Academy is enrichment, not an accredited school or qualification. The safeguarding page is not an approved or operational policy and does not establish that a programme is ready to receive minors. Name responsible safeguarding contacts, confirm procedures and complete qualified local legal review before enrolment or on-site activity involving children.

Liberland's official Constitution page says the Constitution is being drafted. Teach current text, proposals and learning simulations as distinct things. The official page is at https://liberland.org/constitution.
