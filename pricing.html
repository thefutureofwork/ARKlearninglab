What you get

What is a seat?

A seat is one learner, one pathway, for 12 months from your start date.

Included in every seat

  • One pathway: Core Academy, UnSchool or LIBERVERSE Learning Lab
  • The print pack (PDF to print at home)
  • The online version of the same lessons
  • Practical-skills cards
  • A private portfolio folder
  • A parent/guardian guide

Extra seats

  • $200 per learner per year
  • Up to 2 on any plan
  • The extra learner joins the plan's included mentor meetings
  • Same pathway choice and materials as any seat

Rules

  • A learner can switch pathway once a year
  • Seats can't be shared between two learners
  • Unused seats don't carry over
  • Moving up a plan mid-year: you pay only the difference

What happens after you send it

From interest to first lesson.

  1. You register interest.Use the calculator above and send the copied summary. You join the list for when enrolment opens.
  2. Enrolment opens, we send a draft agreement.It lists your plan, seats, pathways, where lessons happen and the price. Nothing is charged at this stage.
  3. Safety and consent forms.The parent/guardian completes consent and contact details. See safeguarding.
  4. Payment.Through a secure payment page sent with your agreement, never through this website.
  5. Materials and kickoff.You receive the print pack, online access and a link to book your first mentor meeting. Then the first lesson starts at home, online or in your pod.
