Three learning formats

The group sizes and support below are proposals, not current bookings.

One Learner

One learner with a parent or guardian, at home with a print pack or online.

Proposed mentoring: two 30-minute video meetings, at kickoff and mid-year, with the learner and parent or guardian.

mini Pod

Up to four learners from one or more families, with one lead parent or guardian. Meet at home or online.

Proposed mentoring: two 30-minute group video meetings for the pod and its parents or guardians.

full Pod

Up to ten learners with one named adult facilitator. Meet online or in a venue the facilitator arranges.

Proposed support: a 60-minute facilitator workshop, a guide and two 45-minute facilitator meetings.

The draft formats allow up to two additional learners per group, subject to safeguarding and staffing.

What each learner would get

  • One pathway: Core Academy, UnSchool or LIBERVERSE Learning Lab.
  • Print and online lesson materials, practical-skills cards and a parent or guardian guide.
  • A private portfolio folder for work and revisions.

Other support under consideration

One-to-one mentoring

Two short online meetings a month, with written follow-up and a parent or guardian nearby.

Learning plan setup

A guided planning meeting, then a short check-in four weeks later. The free learning-plan builder is available now.

Portfolio review

Written feedback from a mentor and a short online call. This service is not open.

Schools and larger groups

The draft school format brings all three pathways into one site, with teacher guides, online training and check-ins.

What you can try now

Enrolment is not open. Try the Danube activity, make a learning plan, or read what needs to be in place before launch.
