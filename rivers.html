Fair-comparison rule.

Discharge is how much water passes a gauge each second, in m³/s (1 m³ = 1,000 litres). Compare raw numbers only when gauge, units, method and period line up. Otherwise compare patterns: how flow grows downstream and how much it swings.

Sources & limits

Where these numbers come from.

Gauge figures were retrieved on 24 Nov 2026 from Wikipedia river articles, which cite ICPDR (Danube), USGS (Mississippi), SO-HYBAM/ANA (Amazon) and the Mekong River Commission State of the Basin report (Mekong). The Nile series is the Aswan annual flow record, 1871 to 1970 (Cobb 1978), a public-domain dataset. Station positions are approximate town locations for mapping.
