Learners must be able to learn, raise a concern and say no to an activity without pressure. Adults stay accountable for supervision, safety and lawful operation.

Commitments

Six promises.

Clear adult accountability

Katherine Tosi is the named safeguarding lead. An alternate and a private reporting process still need to be established before enrolment.

Safe, visible relationships

A code of conduct for mentors and facilitators covering one-to-one contact, messages, boundaries and supervision.

Consent with real choice

Taking part, photos and public sharing of work are separate choices. A learner who opts out gets an equal, safe alternative.

Privacy by default

Collect only what is needed. Limit access, set deletion dates, never publish identifiable learner details, portfolios or learning plans.

Access for everyone

Ask what adjustments help. Offer indoor, low-cost and screen-free options. A camera is never required.

Pause when unsafe

Any responsible adult can stop an activity if conditions change: weather, water, tools, wildlife or behaviour.

  4. Close the loop.Record incidents privately, tell parents/guardians appropriately, and review what should change.

Listen · protect · review

If there is a concern.

If a learner is in immediate danger, act to make them safe and call local emergency services. For a non-urgent concern, send Katherine Tosi a LinkedIn message; the expected reply time is within 48 hours. Do not include sensitive learner details in your first message. Ask for a private follow-up. An alternate route is still to be named before enrolment opens.

  1. Listen calmly.Take the learner seriously. Don't promise secrecy. Don't investigate.
  2. Protect first.Pause the activity or remove the immediate risk. Contact the safeguarding lead. If the concern is about the lead, the alternate contact still needs to be named before enrolment opens.
  3. Record the facts only.Use the private record process. Never share details in group chats.
  4. Escalate.Follow the local procedure for informing parents/guardians and any outside referral. The interim LinkedIn route is not an emergency service.
  5. Review fairly.Someone independent reviews non-urgent decisions where possible. Protection does not wait for an appeal.
