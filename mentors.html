Mentors · always online

A mentor is the learner's guide, not their babysitter.

Every plan includes online meetings with a Liberland Academy mentor. Here is what a mentor does, how long each meeting lasts, and who the mentors are.

What a mentor is

A trained educator who meets you online.

A mentor is an experienced educator from Liberland Academy. The mentor helps the learner set goals, checks the work in the portfolio, gives feedback and helps the parent/guardian or facilitator plan the next steps. The mentor does not teach every lesson: the lessons are in the pack and online.

The learner

Does the lessons, makes choices about projects and evidence, files work in the portfolio and comes to meetings ready to show one thing.

The parent/guardian

Sets up time and space, keeps the learner safe, checks work is filed and joins the meetings. In a mini Pod one lead parent/guardian runs the sessions.

The mentor

Sets goals with you, reviews evidence, gives written feedback, suggests next steps and flags any concern to the safeguarding lead.

Safety rules for every meeting

Meetings are online only, on an approved video platform or by phone. Cameras are optional. For learners under 18, a parent/guardian is present or nearby and can always join. Mentors never ask for private contact details, never move a learner to another app and never meet a learner in person outside an approved ARK day. See safeguarding.

Future of work & systems thinking

At NYU Shanghai, Katherine designed and teaches Future Focused, an interdisciplinary course on how technology and AI are changing work. Students examine employability, workplace change, ethics, inequality and communication, then investigate a local business through research or an interview. Her systems-thinking course uses causal-loop analysis and a research-interview capstone.

Katherine also co-created The 7 Continuums of Learner Agency with Kathleen McClaskey. The framework traces learner growth across voice, choice, engagement, motivation, ownership, purpose and self-efficacy, from teacher-centered practice toward learner-driven agency.

Mentors: all pathways, learning plans, inclusion and English as an additional language. Meets: online only.
