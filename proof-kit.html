LIBERVERSE · Proof Kit · working prototype

Prove the work. Keep the learner private.

Sign a learner’s portfolio after parent consent, print a certificate anyone can check offline, revoke it if consent is withdrawn, and put only an anonymous fingerprint on Ethereum.

Prototype, not for real learners yet

The starting signing key is public, so replace it under Keys before any real use. Ethereum Sepolia (a test network) stands in for Liberland Blockchain V2 until it launches. Files are fingerprinted in your browser and never uploaded; records stay in this browser.
