Family Seat · mini Pod

A small learning group for one household. Up to four learners follow the shared curriculum at their own age level. A parent/guardian partners with the learner; a mentor helps set goals, review work and agree next steps.

At a glance

One household. One simple plan.

Who it's for

One household with up to four learners, ages 6–18. Each learner follows the age-appropriate pathway and shared outcomes.

What's included

Six modules and a capstone for each learner's age band, print-friendly materials, online access, a parent/guardian guide and private portfolio prompts.

Where and when

At home or online, at a flexible pace. Suggested sequence: 36 weeks of modules plus a 4-week capstone. Learners can pause and resume.

What is a mentor?

A mentor is an experienced learning partner. They help a learner set a clear goal, look at work and progress, give useful feedback, and agree a practical next step with the learner and parent/guardian. A mentor does not replace the parent/guardian, the learner's teacher, or formal school or clinical support.

Who does what?

Learner: chooses a goal, brings work and decides what to try next. Parent/guardian: helps make time, shares relevant context and supports the routine. Mentor: asks questions, reviews evidence, gives feedback and records next steps.
