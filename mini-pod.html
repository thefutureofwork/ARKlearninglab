Liberland Academy · Family offer

Family Seat · mini Pod

A small learning group for one household. Up to four learners follow the same shared curriculum at their own age level. A parent/guardian is the learning partner; a mentor helps the family stay on track.

Draft offer · not yet for sale

At a glance

One household. One simple plan.

Who it's for

One household with up to four learners, ages 6–18. Each learner follows the age-appropriate pathway and outcomes.

What is included

All six modules and the capstone for each learner's age band, print-friendly materials, online access, a parent/guardian guide and private portfolio prompts.

Where and when

At home or online. Flexible pace. The suggested sequence is 36 weeks of modules plus a 4-week capstone. Learners can pause and resume.

What mentoring looks like

Meetings have a set length and a clear purpose. They are supportive planning and progress conversations, not therapy, diagnosis or formal assessment.

Who does what

The learner leads; adults support.

The learner chooses a goal, brings their work and decides what to try next. The parent/guardian protects learning time and shares relevant context. The mentor teaches, asks questions and helps the group agree a next step. The mentor does not replace the parent/guardian or make formal decisions for the learner.

All mentions use learner or student, not child/children. Meetings are planning and learning-support conversations, not clinical services. Local safeguarding and privacy requirements must be confirmed before enrolment.
