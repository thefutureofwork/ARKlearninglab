Learning plans · free · for parents/guardians

One page. Up to three goals. Checked every lesson.

A learning plan says what the learner is working towards and how you will know they got there. This guide walks you through it in 10 small steps. It takes about 30 minutes.

First, five words

You only need these.

GoalWhat the learner will be able to do.
BaselineWhat they can do today.
EvidenceThe thing that proves it: a chart, a talk, a model.
SupportWho helps, and how.
ReviewA quick check: on track, or change something?
Example: Mila, age 10, One Learner plan, learns at home.

Goal: explain a Danube water chart in three sentences. Baseline: reads the title only. Evidence: a one-minute recorded explanation. Support: her parent/guardian asks “what goes up? what goes down?” after each lesson. Review: after lessons 4 and 8.

The 10 steps

Do them in order.

Part A · before the first lesson (about 30 minutes, once)

  1. Sit down together.Learner and parent/guardian, 30 minutes, no screens needed.
  2. Pick the pathway.Core Academy, UnSchool or LIBERVERSE. Compare them.
  3. Pick where.Home with the print pack, online, or your pod's venue. Write it on the plan.
  4. Write the baseline.Ask the learner to try one task from lesson 1. Write down what they can do now.
  5. Write 1 to 3 goals.Start with one. Each goal needs evidence and a “by when”.

Part B · every lesson (2 minutes)

  1. Fill one line of the lesson log.Date, done or not, what was made, one note.
  2. Ask one question.“What did you make today?” That's enough.

Part C · every 4 lessons (10 minutes)

  1. Look at the evidence.Open the portfolio. Is the goal getting closer?
  2. Decide: keep, change or finish.Too hard? Make it smaller. Done? Write a new goal.
  3. Share it at the next mentor meeting.Bring the plan to your online meeting. The mentor helps adjust it.
Stuck? Common problems
  • The learner won't start: do the first 10 minutes together, then step back.
  • We missed a week: carry on from the next lesson. Nothing is timed.
  • The goal is too big: cut it in half.
  • I don't understand the topic: you don't have to. Ask the learner to teach you, and bring questions to the mentor.
Checklist before lesson 1
  • Pathway chosen
  • Place chosen (home, online or pod venue)
  • Baseline written
  • At least one goal with evidence and a date
  • Lesson log printed or open
  • First mentor meeting booked

Learner agency · self-check

How much of the plan does the learner write?

The 7 Continuums of Learner Agency map Voice, Choice, Engagement, Motivation, Ownership, Purpose and Self-Efficacy across three stages: teacher-centered, learner-centered and learner-driven. Do the self-check together, then let the learner take over the parts of the plan where they are already learner-driven.
