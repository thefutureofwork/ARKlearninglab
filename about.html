Part 1 · What Liberland is

A country project on the Danube.

2015Declared on 13 April 2015 by Vít Jedlička and Jana Markovičová.
≈ 7 km²About the size of a small town, on the west bank of the Danube.
≈ 1,000Citizens, by Liberland's own count, living around the world.
0UN member states that recognise it as a country.
  1. Where it is.Between Croatia and Serbia, at about 45°46′N 18°52′E, in an area called Gornja Siga. The nearest towns are Zmajevac (Croatia) and Bački Monoštor (Serbia).
  2. Why that land, and who disagrees.Croatia and Serbia have not yet agreed their border along the river. Liberland says this pocket of land is claimed by neither country and was terra nullius (no man's land), so its founders declared a state there. Croatia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs says the area is a delimitation zone between Croatia and Serbia, under Croatian border police control, that it is not terra nullius, and that visitors must follow Croatian and EU law. Learners study both positions.
  3. What it believes.Its motto is “To Live and Let Live”. It focuses on personal freedom, voluntary cooperation, free markets, low taxes and digital government (including its own blockchain).
  4. How it is run today.Liberland describes a provisional government chaired by its president. Its Constitution is still being drafted; the Articles of the Provisional Government apply in the meantime.
  5. What it cannot do yet.The Croatian police restrict access to the land. Liberland says small groups, mostly campers and people living on boats, have stayed there since 2023. It has signed memoranda with some parties abroad, but formal recognition by UN member states is still missing.

Sources: liberland.org/about, liberland.org/constitution and the Croatian Ministry of Foreign Affairs statement “On Virtual Narratives at Croatia’s Borders” (June 2015). Liberland's figures are its own and may change.

Part 2 · Why this school exists

Because the questions Liberland asks are the questions every learner should learn to answer.

1. A real case study

Most learners only meet rights, rules and markets in a textbook. Liberland is a live example of people designing them from scratch. Learners can study real documents, real debates and real trade-offs.

2. Thinking, not believing

We teach learners to sort fact, claim, proposal and opinion. They study Liberland's ideas critically, compare them with other countries and draw their own conclusions.

3. Freedom with responsibility

“To Live and Let Live” becomes classroom practice: learners make real choices about projects and evidence, and take responsibility for the result.

4. Digital life done well

Liberland is a digital-first project. Our LIBERVERSE Learning Lab teaches privacy, data, code and AI so learners can use technology safely and build with it.

5. A community with no schools

Liberland's citizens and supporters live all over the world. There is no Liberland school building, so we built one that works at home, online and in small pods.

6. The river as a classroom

The Danube links the science lessons (water, habitats, energy, food) to the civics lessons (maps, borders, claims). ARK Village in Apatin, Serbia, on the same river, will host optional visit days.

Our mission

Help learners aged 6 to 18 become free, careful thinkers who can weigh evidence, make fair rules, trade honestly and build useful things, at home, online or together in pods.

Part 3 · What this school is not

Clear limits.

Not a state school

Liberland Academy is not an official institution of the Liberland government and does not speak for it.

Not accredited

It is an enrichment programme. It does not award recognised diplomas. Families follow their own local education rules.

Not on Liberland's land

No lesson takes place on the territory itself. Lessons happen at home, online, in pods and, later, at ARK Village in Serbia.

Where do the lessons happen?

Most learning happens where the learner already lives. You choose the place. Every seat includes both the print pack and online access, so you can mix them.

Place 1 · most common

At home

The learner works at your kitchen table or desk from the printed pack. A parent/guardian sets up the time and checks the work.

Place 2

Online, anywhere

The same lessons as short online modules on a computer or tablet, at home or travelling. Self-paced. No live class to attend.

Place 3 · pods only

Your pod's own venue

A mini Pod meets in the lead parent/guardian's home. A full Pod meets in a room its facilitator arranges (a home, library or hall). We send the materials; we do not provide the room.

Place 4 · optional, later

ARK Village, Apatin, Serbia

Optional hands-on days at ARK Village, Mirni Dunav 3, Apatin, Serbia, on the Danube. Not open yet: booking starts only after safeguarding sign-off. Travel and stays are extra.

Mentoring meetings are always online (video, or phone if you prefer). No lessons take place on Liberland's own land: access to that land is restricted by the Croatian police, so the school is built to work at home and online. What is Liberland?
