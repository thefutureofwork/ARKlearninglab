ARK Learning Lab · River Data Pack (Unit 01)
Use these files offline. No website visit is needed.

FILES
1. gauges_downstream.csv  : 27 gauges on 4 rivers (Danube, Mississippi, Amazon, Mekong), upstream to downstream. Discharge in cubic metres per second (m3/s). Min/max are recorded extremes; blank = not reported in this source.
2. danube_annual_2000_2023.csv : yearly mean discharge at Reni/Isaccea (near the Black Sea) and Batina/Bezdan (near ARK Village, Apatin).
3. nile_aswan_annual_1871_1970.csv : yearly Nile volume at Aswan, in units of 100 million m3, plus an approximate mean flow in m3/s (volume / seconds in a year). Public-domain series (Cobb 1978), distributed with the statsmodels library.

HOW TO READ
- Discharge = how much water passes a gauge each second. 1 m3 is a cube of water 1 m on each side (1,000 litres).
- Lat/lon are approximate town positions for mapping, not exact gauge coordinates.
- Periods differ between stations and rivers. Compare raw numbers only within one river and similar periods; across rivers, compare patterns (how flow grows downstream, how much it varies).
