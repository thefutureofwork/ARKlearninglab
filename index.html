1 · What ARK solves

What does ARK help your child do?

“My child needs structure, but a one-size-fits-all course isn’t working.”

Choose a structured pathway or a learner-led project. Start with a clear goal, break it into steps and use a one-page learning plan to track what happens next.

“They finish worksheets, but I can’t see what they really understand.”

Learners make something: a map, graph, model, explanation or presentation. They collect it in a portfolio so you can see the work, not just a score.

“I want learning to connect to real life.”

In the draft River Pilot, learners read river data, investigate a question and develop a recommendation. The Danube is one of five featured rivers. The pilot is designed to run online, not at ARK Village, and no cohort is open yet.

“My child has interests that don’t fit neatly into a subject.”

A learner might explore water use, food, digital privacy or a community problem. They choose a question and how to present their findings, while an adult helps set goals and checkpoints.

“I want them to understand technology, not just use it.”

The LIBERVERSE materials introduce privacy, data, coding and AI through age-banded activities. Learners can use writing, drawing, speaking or digital work to show their thinking.

“I need to know what my role is.”

Parents and guardians help choose goals, set a routine, supervise where needed and review the work. The free plan builder gives you a place to start. Mentor support is part of the draft model, not yet an available service.

3 · The portfolio

Five pieces of work. One clear record of learning.

Every pathway builds the same five-piece portfolio. The examples below change with the age band you picked above.

  1. 1

    Question & map

    What am I trying to find out, and where?

    Navigators exampleAn annotated river map and one question

  2. 2

    Observations & data

    What did I notice, count or measure?

    Navigators exampleA bar chart made from real gauge data

  3. 3

    Design or build

    What did I make, test or write?

    Navigators exampleA tested soil or water-saving design

  4. 4

    Feedback & revision

    What advice did I get, and what did I change?

    Navigators exampleMentor feedback and the change I made

  5. 5

    Explain & reflect

    What did I learn, and what can’t my evidence prove?

    Navigators exampleA three-sentence explanation of what I found

Any format countsDrawing, writing, speaking, a model, a photo of a build, code or a chart. A camera is never required.
An adult checks each pieceA parent, guardian or pod facilitator ticks it off. Every four lessons, you review it together.
Private by defaultUse a nickname. The portfolio builder saves only on your device. Nothing is published.

Badges, not money.

A finished five-piece portfolio earns a printable certificate for that age band. Learners never get crypto, tokens or Liberland Merits. The Proof Kit can let someone check the work later without seeing your child’s name.

4 · ARK River Pilot · six-week draft · online

Six weeks. One river. One recommendation.

A draft online project using real river data. The Danube is one test river; learners can also choose the Mississippi, Amazon, Nile, Mekong or a river near home. The plan is one 90-minute online session a week, plus an optional project block at home. No cohort is open yet.

Questions families ask

No pressure to enrol. Start with the details.

What does a normal week look like?

It depends on the pathway: Core Academy is one or two lessons of 60–90 minutes, UnSchool is roughly 2–4 project hours, and LIBERVERSE is about 90 minutes. Each session ends with one piece of work filed in the portfolio. You spend about two minutes a session checking in, and ten minutes every fourth lesson reviewing the portfolio together.

Can children earn Liberland Merits or crypto?

No. Learners never get crypto wallets, tokens, payments or Liberland Merits. A finished portfolio earns a printable certificate for the learner’s age band instead.

Does the river test run at ARK Village?

No. The ARK River Pilot is designed to run online, and no cohort is open yet. The Danube is one test river among five. ARK Village in Serbia is a possible future option only.

Can we buy a plan today?

No. Prices are drafts only. The site does not accept applications, payment details or enrolments.

What is the Proof Kit?

A working prototype for checking portfolio work. A facilitator can sign a learner’s work after parent consent, print a certificate and let anyone check it later. Files never leave the browser. Open the Proof Kit.

How do I give feedback?

Email kat@arklearninglab.com. Tell us what you tried, what was clear and what got in the way. Please don’t include your child’s full name or school. Safeguarding concerns follow the safeguarding page.
