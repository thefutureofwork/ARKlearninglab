What Liberland is, in 30 seconds

A small country project with a big idea: “To Live and Let Live”.

Liberland was declared on 13 April 2015 on about 7 km² of land on the Danube, between Croatia and Serbia, that neither country claims. It has around 1,000 citizens, a provisional government and a constitution still being drafted. No United Nations member state recognises it.

Why a school?

Liberland asks hard questions: what makes a rule fair, who owns what, how should a digital community govern itself? Those are great questions for learners. Liberland Academy turns them into lessons, using Liberland as a real case study, not as propaganda. Read the full story and why this school exists.

Where

Where do the lessons happen?

Most learning happens where the learner already lives. You choose the place. Every seat includes both the print pack and online access, so you can mix them.

Place 1 · most common

At home

The learner works at your kitchen table or desk from the printed pack. A parent/guardian sets up the time and checks the work.

Place 2

Online, anywhere

The same lessons as short online modules on a computer or tablet, at home or travelling. Self-paced. No live class to attend.

Place 3 · pods only

Your pod's own venue

A mini Pod meets in the lead parent/guardian's home. A full Pod meets in a room its facilitator arranges (a home, library or hall). We send the materials; we do not provide the room.

Place 4 · optional, later

ARK Village, Apatin, Serbia

Optional hands-on days at ARK Village, Mirni Dunav 3, Apatin, Serbia, on the Danube. Not open yet: booking starts only after safeguarding sign-off. Travel and stays are extra.

Mentoring meetings are always online (video, or phone if you prefer). No lessons take place on Liberland's own land: access to that land is restricted by the Croatian police, so the school is built to work at home and online. What is Liberland?

How to join

Five steps, in this order.

  1. Count your learners.1 or 2 learners: One Learner. 3 to 6: mini Pod. 7 to 12: full Pod. 13 or more: school licence. The price calculator picks the cheapest plan for you.
  2. Choose a pathway for each learner.Academy, UnSchool or LIBERVERSE. Not sure? Start with Academy; you can switch once.
  3. Choose where they will learn.At home with the print pack, online, or both. Pods also use their own venue.
  4. Add extras if you want them.1:1 mentoring, a Learning Plan Setup meeting or a Portfolio Review. All optional.
  5. Send your enrolment request.Copy your summary from the calculator and send it to the founding mentor. Enrolment is not open yet, so no payment is taken.

Straight answers

Who, what, where, when, why, how.

Who is it for?

Learners (students) aged 6 to 18, their parent/guardian, and pod facilitators. A mentor from Liberland Academy supports each plan online. Meet the mentors.

What does the learner do?

One lesson at a time, usually 60 to 90 minutes. Each lesson ends with something the learner made: a chart, a model, a rule, a short talk. The work goes into a private folder (the portfolio).

Where do lessons take place?

At home, online, or at your pod's own venue. Optional day visits at ARK Village, Apatin, Serbia will open later. Mentoring is always online. Nothing takes place on Liberland's own land.

When?

Start any month. A seat lasts 12 months from your start date. We suggest one lesson a week. Mentor meetings are booked at times that suit your time zone.

Why Liberland?

Because it is a living example of people trying to design rights, rules, money and digital government from scratch. Learners study it critically: what is fact, what is a claim, what is still a proposal. Why this school exists.

How do we pay?

Not yet. Prices on this site are drafts. When enrolment opens you will pay per year, or in 3 instalments (5% extra). This website never asks for card details.

Is it a real school?

It is an enrichment programme, not an accredited school, and not an official institution of the Liberland government. Check your local home-education rules.
