Liberland Science & Civics Pack

32 lessons. Eight units. Six stories.

Proposed planning baseline: four lessons per unit. One common core across print, online and ARK. Learners choose a question, role and way to show what they know.

01

Hydrology + data

The Danube as a Living System

Water movement, observation and what a sample cannot prove.

02

Ecology + sampling

Danube Neighbours

Habitat needs, biodiversity counts and low-disturbance surveys.

03

Physics + design

Energy, Materials & Circular Design

Energy, material lifecycles and the case for repair or reuse.

04

Life science + systems

Food, Soil & Local Resilience

Soil, plant needs and fair tests, from seed to food choices.

05

Geography + source literacy

Mapping Place & Competing Claims

Maps, regional geography and claims carefully distinguished.

06

Civics + due process

Rights, Rules & Fair Decisions

Consent, rights and rules that can be explained and reviewed.

07

Economics + stewardship

Markets, Property & the Commons

Scarcity, incentives, ownership and shared-resource trade-offs.

08

Digital systems + privacy

Digital Civics, Data & Consent

Identity, data and governance through paper-first simulations.

Audio stories
